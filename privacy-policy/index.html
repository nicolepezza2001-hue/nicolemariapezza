Privacy Policy

This site is nicolemariapezza.com, the author website of Nicole Maria Pezza.

What we collect

If you enter your email address in the preorder form, it is sent to the author by email so you can be told when the novel is available. It is used for nothing else and is never sold or shared.

Services this site uses

Form submissions are delivered through FormSubmit. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services may log basic technical information such as your IP address, as any web server does.

Visitor statistics

The site counts visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service. It sets no cookies, does not track you across sites and stores no personal data: only anonymous totals such as which pages are viewed, the country, the browser and screen size, and whether the first chapter is opened. This site sets no cookies at all.

Your rights

To have your email address removed, or to ask what we hold about you, write to nicolemariapezza@gmail.com.
